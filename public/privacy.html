Privacy Policy, Terms & Accessibility

1. Data Controller Identity & Contact Details

The Data Controller responsible for this personal portfolio website is Adam Nordling. Inquiries regarding data privacy can be directed via GitHub security advisory or LinkedIn.

2. Data Protection Officer (DPO) Contact

Given the personal nature of this portfolio and absence of large-scale processing under Article 37 GDPR, a designated Data Protection Officer is not required. All data privacy inquiries are handled directly by the Data Controller.

3. Legal Basis for Processing & Data Retention Period

This website processes minimal technical log data strictly under legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data retention period is strictly ephemeral: connection logs are retained only for real-time edge routing and discarded immediately.

4. Cookie Policy & Tracking Technologies

This website employs zero cookies, zero local storage tracking, and zero advertising or analytics tracking pixels. All digital tools execute 100% client-side inside the visitor's local browser sandbox.

5. Data Subject Rights

Under GDPR, visitors hold data subject rights including the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to object.

6. Recipients of Personal Data & International Data Transfers

There are zero third-party recipients of personal data. Data is never sold, leased, or transferred. Technical network routing is handled via Cloudflare Edge servers under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

7. Right to Lodge a Complaint with a Supervisory Authority

Visitors have the right to lodge a complaint with their supervisory authority, such as the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten — IMY).

8. Terms of Service & Acceptable Use Policy

All open-source source code repositories, research papers, and applications presented on this portfolio are provided for educational demonstration under the MIT License.

10. Accessibility Statement

This website is committed to digital accessibility in accordance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards and the EU Web Accessibility Directive (Directive 2016/2102).
